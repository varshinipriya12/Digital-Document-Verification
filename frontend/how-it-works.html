System Architecture & Workflow

How Verification Works

Understand how OpenCV image processing, Tesseract OCR, and Google Gemini AI work together to ensure your documents are ready before submission.

  1. 1. Create an Account or Sign In

    Sign in using email credentials or Google OAuth to secure your verification sessions.

  2. 2. Select Government Document Verification

    Select the unified Government Document Verification service supporting Aadhaar, PAN, Income, Caste, Residence, Birth Certificate, Passport, and Marksheets.

  3. 3. View Required Documents

    Dynamically fetch official document requirements stored in MongoDB.

  4. 4. Upload Documents

    Upload PDF, JPG, or PNG files (up to 8 MB per file). Files are securely stored with random UUIDs.

  5. 5. OpenCV Document Quality Inspection

    OpenCV calculates Laplacian variance for blur detection, mean brightness, resolution, and blank image checks.

  6. 6. OCR Text Extraction

    PyTesseract performs image thresholding and extracts readable text snippets for evidence matching.

  7. 7. Gemini AI Document-Type & Content Verification

    Google Gemini AI checks whether the uploaded file actually matches the required document type.

  8. 8. Receive Document-Wise Results

    Get itemized status badges (READY, NEEDS ATTENTION, INVALID, MISSING) with confidence scores.

  9. 9. Review Final Verification Report

    Inspect overall session status and actionable recommendations if any document needs attention.

  10. 10. Continue to Official Government Portal

    Once all documents pass as READY, click through to the official portal to complete your official application.

📢 Note: This application provides preliminary document-readiness checks and does not replace official government verification.
